# The Notedown identity

**Observed phase: 11 August 2026**

The recorded transition from Writing Desk to Notedown took place on 11 August. The name change accompanied interface work rather than standing alone: document titles became part of the application chrome, menus were refined, and the identity was carried into the app's visual mark.

The mark is a white slash on a blue rounded square. It connects the name to a useful interaction already present in the editor: typing a slash to open structure commands. A compact mark also suits the application bar and installed-app icon without requiring a detailed illustration.

The surrounding interface became quieter. Work on title treatment and overflow menus reduced competing controls. Reading mode acquired a clearer route back to editing, and the focus experience brought typewriter-style scrolling into a single writing mode. Delete actions gained an explicit confirmation step, while the find and slash controls became easier to recognize.

Tables received particular attention during this phase. Their controls needed to support both selecting or moving the table and editing its contents. Refining grips and edge controls helped distinguish those intentions in a part of the page where an ordinary click can have several meanings.

Together, these changes aimed to make the application feel coherent at the point of use: the name, mark, menus, and writing modes should help a writer understand the workspace.
