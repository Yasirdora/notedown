# Community reading room — planning notes

**Purpose:** Agree on a small weekend trial for the reading room.

**Participants:** Alex, Maya, and Sam.

## What we want to learn

Can a quiet, welcoming space help neighbors spend an hour reading together?
The first trial should answer that question with a simple setup and a short
conversation at the end.

## Decisions

| Topic | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Session length | One hour | Easy to try without committing an afternoon |
| Seating | Two small tables and a quiet corner | Offers a choice of shared or independent space |
| Books | Bring your own; a small shelf is available | Keeps preparation manageable |
| Feedback | Three questions on a paper card | Collects useful reactions without an account |

## Preparation

- [x] Agree on the purpose of the trial

- [ ] Confirm that the room is available — Alex
- [ ] Prepare signs and feedback cards — Maya

### Invitation

> Bring a book and settle in for an hour of quiet reading. You can join the
> closing conversation or simply finish your chapter.

### Room checklist

1. Keep the entrance clear.
2. Place the welcome sign where it is easy to read.
3. Leave a route between tables wide enough to move through comfortably.
4. Put feedback cards near the exit.

## Questions to carry forward

- Is one hour the right length?
- Do people prefer a regular time or occasional sessions?
- What would make the room easier to use?

## Next conversation

Review the feedback after the trial. Decide whether to repeat the same format
before adding activities or expanding the schedule.
