# Evening library hours

*Decision paper · 14 October 2026*

## Recommendation

Approve a ==six-week trial== of Thursday evening opening, from 18:00 to 21:00.
Use existing space and staffing, and review attendance before committing to
a permanent change.

## What the trial will test

The trial will establish whether later opening helps people who cannot visit
during the working day. It will also measure the staffing required and whether
evening use differs from daytime use.

Collect attendance by hour and a short, voluntary response from visitors.
Record practical problems as they occur, including access, lighting, and
closing arrangements.

## Proposed scope

| Element | Proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Opening | Thursdays, 18:00–21:00 |
| Duration | Six consecutive weeks |
| Staffing | Two staff members per session |
| Review | Attendance, visitor feedback, and staff time |
